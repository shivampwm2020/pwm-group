### The Rising Role of Fintech

The asset management industry is undergoing a major transformation. Fintech is reshaping everything from client onboarding to portfolio analytics.

A few months ago, we asked our readers what topic they were most eager to explore. The message was clear — over 50% of our audience wanted to understand how this technological revolution is transforming investment management.

As someone who's witnessed this transformation first-hand, I've seen traditional firms reinvent themselves through technology, while fintech companies continue to challenge the status quo.

Let me share some prominent examples...

---

### Asset Management Areas Disrupted by Fintech

- **Advanced Data Analytics**: Enables asset managers to make informed decisions by uncovering insights from large datasets and optimizing portfolio performance.
- **Regulatory Technology**: Helps asset managers comply with regulations more efficiently by automating processes, reducing errors, and cutting costs.
- **ESG Investing**: Supports the growth of Environmental, Social, and Governance investing by providing tools to measure and report on sustainability and ethical impact.

Fintech is driving inclusivity, efficiency, and transparency across the financial ecosystem.

---

### The Biggest Industry Disruptor

**Big Data & Analytics.**  
Having high-quality, granular data is key — especially when applying AI.

> “Garbage in, garbage out” (GIGO) is never more true, and it highlights the importance of reliable foundational datasets.

Firms that produce and maintain structured, global data can unlock:

- Enhanced decision-making
- Improved risk management
- Personalised investment strategies
- Increased operational efficiency
- Stronger compliance and transparency
- Better client insights
- Scalability and growth

High-quality data is the fuel for AI to deliver personalized, predictive, and scalable asset management solutions.

### The Importance of Understanding Flow Drivers for Sales Teams

Understanding flow drivers is critical for asset management sales teams. It requires analyzing complex data patterns, market dynamics, and client behaviors to gain actionable insight.

Despite advancements in AI, data reconciliation, and analytics, many sales teams still struggle to unlock the full potential of their flow data.

Below are **six key obstacles** commonly faced:

---

### 1. Deciphering Omnibus Flows

Large omnibus and platform flows take significant time to dissect, especially for funds with high redemption risk.

> Sales teams often miss critical engagement windows due to lack of timely visibility.

**PWM’s Trade Explorer** offers deep, actionable insights — no external dashboard setup needed.

---

### 2. Distributor Country Breakouts

Flows from global banks and partners are often presented in **aggregated formats**, with minimal country-level or client-type breakdowns.

This lack of granularity results in:

- Internal sales disputes
- Complex credit attribution
- Poor market alignment

---

### 3. Institutional Cash Flows

Distinguishing **net new investments** from normal portfolio activity is a major challenge — especially post-mandate or during rebalancing.

> Misreading these movements can derail relationship strategies and sales resource allocation.

---

### 4. Sub-Advised Flow Blindspots

Sub-advised partners often offer **limited and inconsistent flow data**. This:

- Limits sales targeting
- Distorts performance attribution
- Hampers country-level reporting

---

### 5. Mismatched Client Flows

Client misattribution in CRMs or Client Master systems is widespread.

Example: Transfer agent accounts mapped to the wrong entity — like attributing flows to **Clearstream** instead of the **actual sub-distributor or DFM**.

This leads to:

- Skewed sales data
- Poor targeting
- Missed upsell opportunities

> **PWM’s Two-Step Matching** combines intuitive account search with structured organization mapping.

---

### 6. Model Portfolio Complexities

Flows influenced by **model portfolio changes** (often made by 3rd-party research teams) can cause:

- Large, coordinated shifts
- Low visibility for sales teams
- Limited opportunity for traditional engagement

These flows require:

- Greater transparency
- Different sales strategies
- Better integration with model providers
